This report covers ten days (2 to 12 November) because we skipped last Monday's edition. The clearest cut is at Printify: five of its six price changes went down, including the Mesh-Back Cap ($21.22 to $15.16), Glass Ornaments ($19.38 to $15.86) and the Unisex Cotton T-Shirt ($12.83 to $10.95). With ornament season starting, Customizingbox's jingle bell ornament also fell from $6.75 to $4.84, and Printway's custom-shape wooden puzzle dropped from $14.00 to $6.00.

The biggest increase is NeatoPOD's ITC Heavyweight Hoodie: $31.95 to $46.95 for S-3XL and $42.95 to $58.95 for 4XL-5XL. That moved the cheapest offer for that hoodie to POD Hut at $43.74, so if you sell it through NeatoPOD, re-check your margin now. Zela Print reshuffled its poster prices: the small sizes went up (A5 from $4.08 to $4.70, 8x10 from $4.22 to $4.70), while 12x18 and 16x20 got cheaper ($5.83 to $5.10 and $6.03 to $5.37).

Across 103 suppliers we counted 53 price changes of 3% or more: 26 cuts and 27 increases. A note on our data: Merchize changed how its product pages publish prices on 11 November, and for one refresh most of its catalog dropped out of our comparisons. That is fixed and all 869 Merchize products are back at unchanged prices. Two suppliers are hidden for now because we could not refresh them for more than a week: OOWIA and Print Partners.
