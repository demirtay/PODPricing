## Açıklama (Hakkında)
Price data for print-on-demand sellers on Etsy, Amazon, Shopify and TikTok Shop.

The same blank can cost twice as much depending on who prints it. Here we share real numbers: what 150+ POD suppliers charge for the same product (print included), supplier bestsellers vs the cheapest alternative, weekly price changes, and sample/quality experiences from members.

Ask about any blank or product and we'll pull the prices. Full comparison tool (free, no sign-up): podpricing.com

## Kurallar
1. **Share real numbers** — When you talk about a supplier, mention product, price and date if you can. Opinions are welcome, data is better.
2. **No spam** — No selling services (VA, design, ads) in posts or comments. Supplier reps are welcome but must say who they are.
3. **Be helpful and civil** — Everyone was a beginner once. No personal attacks.
4. **Prices change** — Base prices exclude shipping and tax. Always order a sample before you list a product.

## Üyelik soruları (isteğe bağlı, spam azaltır)
1. Where do you sell? (Etsy, Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop, other)
2. Which POD supplier do you use most?

Welcome! 👋

This group is about one thing: paying less for the same print-on-demand product.

What you'll find here:

• Weekly price changes ("who got cheaper/pricier this week")
