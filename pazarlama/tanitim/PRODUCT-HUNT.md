```
Hi Product Hunt! 👋

I sell print-on-demand products on Etsy, and every time I picked a supplier I had to open a dozen dashboards to compare base costs. So I built POD Pricing.

What it does:
• Tracks 35,000+ products from 100+ POD suppliers, refreshed twice a day from their public catalogs
• Matches identical products (same blank, like a Gildan 5000 tee, or same type and size, like an 11oz mug) and sorts them cheapest first
• 230+ head-to-head comparisons, e.g. Printful vs Printify on the 144 products both sell
• A profit calculator with Etsy/Shopify/Amazon fees, published shipping and US import duty

One thing that surprised me: for the same product, the most expensive supplier typically charges 73% more than the cheapest.

It's free, no signup. I'd love to hear which suppliers or products you'd like added next.
```

## Lansman günü yorum cevap kalıpları (Claude güncel rakamla doldurur)
- "Where do prices come from?" → Suppliers' public catalogs and price lists, refreshed twice a day; every listing shows when it was checked.
- "Do you take commission?" → Some supplier links are affiliate links; ranking is by price only, never changed by affiliates.
- "Can you add X?" → Yes if X publishes prices publicly; we add every supplier with open pricing.
