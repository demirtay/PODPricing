## 1) Reddit (r/printondemand, r/EtsySellers) — kural: reklam değil veri; bağlantı yorumda istenirse
Başlık: Same Gildan 5000 tee, same $24.99 Etsy price: profit ranged from $1.42 to $11.63 depending on the POD supplier

Gövde:
I was tired of comparing base prices only, so I added up everything for a US buyer: base cost, the supplier's own published US shipping rate, import duty (0 when it ships from the US) and Etsy fees (6.5% + 3% + $0.25 + $0.20 listing).

Gildan 5000, sold at $24.99, buyer in the US:
- BurgerPrints: $5.39 + $5.15 shipping → $11.63 profit
- Dreamship: $5.39 + $5.19 → $11.59
- Merchize: $5.25 + $5.90 → $11.02
- SimplePrint: $6.72 + $4.99 → $10.46
- Printify (Printful facility): $8.55 + $4.95 → $8.67
- Printful: $9.44 + $4.95 → $7.78
- NeatoPOD: $10.95 + $6.59 → $4.63
- Prodigi: $10.00 + $10.75 → $1.42

Same blank, same listing price: the supplier choice alone is a ~$10 difference per sale. On mugs it's worse: one supplier actually loses money at $18.99 once shipping is included.

Shipping is the part most comparisons skip. Happy to share the full table for other products if anyone wants it.
