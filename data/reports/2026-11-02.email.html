The biggest move this week is on blankets. Interestprint cut its US-fulfilled (Queens) Ultra-Soft Micro Fleece Blankets by 22-28% across six sizes: the 30x40 went from $8.59 to $6.19, the 50x70 from $14.99 to $11.19 and the 60x70 from $15.49 to $11.99. Blanket listings for the holidays are going up now, so check the blanket comparisons before you lock in a supplier.

Drinkware and ornaments got cheaper too. Artsadd more than halved its steel tumblers: the 40oz tumbler with handle dropped from $33.34 to $14.20 and the 30oz from $23.19 to $11.88. Dreamship’s wooden ornaments (round maple and custom shape) fell from about $7.40 to $4.69, and Customizingbox’s star photo ornament went from $6.51 to $2.80 — worth a look if you sell personalised ornaments this season.

Across 103 suppliers we counted 49 price changes of 3% or more: 41 cuts and 8 increases. The increases were small and scattered, the largest being PrintKK’s canvas document bag ($2.86 to $4.13) and PeaPrint’s double-layered all-over-print hoodie ($16.99 to $19.56). From this week we no longer count a price that changed for a day or two and then went back: Printful’s public catalog briefly quoted different prices around 25-27 October, and those were not real price changes.
