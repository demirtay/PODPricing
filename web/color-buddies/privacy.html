Color Buddies Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Color Buddies is a coloring game for children. We built it so that it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way for a child to type or send anything.

What stays on the device

Pictures your child colors are saved only on the device, inside the app, so they can come back and continue. They never leave the device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Ads, analytics and third parties

The app shows no ads, has no in-app purchases, and contains no analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.

Permissions

The app does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

Children

The app is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), because no data is collected from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Use the developer contact on the app's Google Play page.
