Pour Sort Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Pour Sort is a colour sorting puzzle game. This page explains what information is used when you play.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. We do not run our own servers that store information about you.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, daily challenge medals and streak are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Ads

The app can show a video ad only when you choose to watch one, for an extra undo or an extra tube. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under the Google partner sites policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

Pour Sort is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
