This first weekly report covers October 12 to October 19, 2026. Out of more than 35,000 products we track, 65 print-included prices moved by 3% or more. Drops outnumbered increases almost three to one (48 vs 17), which is what you would expect heading into Q4: suppliers compete for the holiday orders that are about to land.

The biggest mover was Interestprint, which cut 25 prices by 17% on average. Most of the cuts hit its US-shipped canvas prints: the 12" x 12" inner-frame canvas went from $10.29 to $6.69, and the 8" x 10" from $6.99 to $5.29. If you sell wall art for Christmas, canvas margins just got noticeably better. Interestprint also became the cheapest supplier for 24" x 36" posters, though only by two cents over Zela Print.

Printful went the other way on its organic Stanley/Stella line. The Crafter tee rose from $11.25 to $14.56 and the Drummer 2.0 hoodie from $28.50 to $36.40, increases of 22% to 29%. If those blanks are in your Etsy or Shopify listings, check your retail prices this week, because the same margin now needs a higher price. PopCustoms and CustomizingBox trimmed prices on personalized gifts, and Printify's moves were mixed: wood signs got 34% cheaper while the Tritan water bottle got 16% more expensive.
