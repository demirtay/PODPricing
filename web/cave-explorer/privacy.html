Cave Explorer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Cave Explorer (Mağara Kaşifi) is an adventure game for children about exploring a cave, mapping it and discovering chemical elements. This page explains what information is used when your child plays.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way for a child to type or send personal information. We do not run servers that store information about players.

What stays on the device

Game progress (depth reached, gold, elements found, map progress, settings) is saved only on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Ads

The game can show a short video ad only when the player chooses to watch one, for example to continue after falling. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a Google Play Families certified ad network. Every ad request is marked as directed to children (COPPA) and under the age of consent, and limited to content rated for general audiences. This means ads are not personalised, no advertising ID is read by the app, and the ad network uses only the minimum technical information needed to deliver an ad, such as the device type and IP address, as described in the Google partner sites policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app only uses internet access to load ads. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

Children

The game is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
